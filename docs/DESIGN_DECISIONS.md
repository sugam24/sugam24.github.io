# Design decisions

## A quiet academic and engineering portfolio

The cream paper and burgundy accents establish an editorial feel suited to both research and engineering work. Serif headings mark changes in subject, while readable sans-serif body text carries the detail. Thin separators and left-aligned paragraphs let readers scan without needing cards or decorative graphics. The numbered sidebar and lowercase `sd.` monogram are the site's visual signatures.

## Navigation and first impression

The fixed desktop sidebar gives readers a persistent map of a long page. The mobile drawer preserves the same nine labels and order. Research Experience and Publications sit early because they answer different questions. Research Experience explains the work and findings, and Publications gives direct access to scholarly outputs. Professional Experience and Selected Projects follow before Education, Technical Skills, Credentials & Recognition, and Beyond the Lab.

The hero establishes who Sugam is and what he works on. Its top masthead intentionally shows Kathmandu, Nepal and 2026. The portrait has no caption because location already appears in the masthead and contact block. Its crop and alignment are part of the composition. The pronunciation control gives visitors a discreet way to hear the name.

Selected Highlights offers three quick paths to the preprint, dataset, and LightOnOCR project. Northeast arrows indicate external resources, while the downward arrow indicates an in-page jump. The highlights stay compact so the hero can lead into the portfolio.

## How the evidence is presented

- The four featured projects and two additional projects are the current editorial baseline. Featured projects have room for implementation details. Additional projects use a more compact grid that stacks on small screens.
- Education presents the undergraduate qualification first and gives it stronger typographic emphasis. Dates and results sit beside the relevant institution and qualification.
- The Technical Skills panel uses the sidebar's soft background color and a small set of grouped lists. Burgundy technology stacks distinguish project technologies from prose.
- Credentials & Recognition starts with the professional affiliation, followed by the selected credentials. The registration-number line uses the owner's approved placeholder until the number is provided.
- Beyond the Lab closes the page in a personal voice and uses the same quiet paragraph treatment as the rest of the site.

## Small controls and contact details

Contact information stays in the sidebar for quick access. The muted NPT clock provides useful context for readers in other time zones. Email and CV access appear in both the hero and sidebar so readers can find them at entry and while browsing.

The circular theme toggle fits the masthead without drawing attention away from the name. The site opens in light mode and remembers an explicit dark preference. The footer's last-updated date gives readers context about the portfolio's currency. Its Back to top link sits at the end of the page, where it can return readers to the hero without introducing a floating control.
